Implemented code:
- src/core/packet.hpp/.cpp bounds frame lengths and applies minimum 60-byte zero
  padding and exact RTL8125 UDP/PTP rules (ports319/320, patch47, packet<175,
  short UDP header8). Checked IPv4 IHL/options/fragment offsets and direct IPv6
  UDP parsing; VLAN/QinQ insertion is rejected. Original bytes are preserved.
- src/reactos/datapath.hpp/.cpp gathers at most256 NDIS_BUFFERs into owned copied
  storage. NdisQueryPacket requests only FirstBuffer, avoiding its unbounded
  total-length walk. Safe MDL mapping and checked cumulative lengths prevent
  overflow; zero-length cycles terminate. Explicit checksum/TSO/VLAN metadata
  requests are rejected: protocol software finishes checksums/segmentation.
- Serialized producer/consumer/pending-slot ownership uses existing DmaOwner.
  OWN is published by that owner after payload; checked 16-bit TxPoll0x90=1
  follows publication. Exactly one pending NDIS packet belongs to each live
  slot. Ring-full backpressure returns RESOURCES and notifies availability once
  after progress. Completion clears state before the NDIS callback, tolerating
  synchronous resubmission. Duplicate pending pointers are rejected.
- Post-OWN doorbell failure keeps PENDING ownership and faults admission; it
  never rolls back live DMA. stop_datapath only stops CPU admission. Abort/free
  refuse until the separate DMA owner positively retires and callbacks finish.

Validation completed:
- GCC15.2 host ASan/UBSan: 8,335 new packet/filter checks, zero failures. Existing
  core1,555 checks also pass after unchanged receive decoder extraction.
  ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0 (process tracing prevents LeakSanitizer).
- GCC13 MinGW i386 + genuine matched ReactOS SDK: contract executable and a
  combined kernel-link .sys build. PE imports only ndis.sys/hal.dll/ntoskrnl.exe.
  The combined .sys is a link check, not the published probe or an active driver.
- Wine real-SDK fixture with mocked NDIS/NT imports and memory-only NIC:

  native short/PTP padding, reentrant submit, no double completion, failed
  doorbell retention, modeled retirement and exactly-once error completion.
  It executes no real DMA, PCI, MMIO, kernel or network traffic.
